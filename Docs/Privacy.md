# Still Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Still is provided by Ether LLC. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data stored by the app

Still stores completed pause sessions, including their start time, end time, and
duration, in the app’s local SwiftData store on your device. The app does not
include an analytics SDK, advertising SDK, account system, remote content
database, or photo-library integration.

## On-device assistant

On supported Apple devices and languages, assistant requests use Apple Foundation
Models on device. Still does not send prompts or generated responses to a remote
AI provider or to Ether LLC. Model availability and system-level diagnostics are
controlled by the operating system and Apple’s applicable terms.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases. Still requests localized product information and reads
cryptographically verified StoreKit transactions to decide whether Pro access is
active. Ether LLC does not receive or store your payment-card details in the app.

## Retention and deletion

Local content remains on the device until you delete it in Still, remove the app,
or erase the device. Deleting an item removes it from the app’s local store.
StoreKit transaction history is retained by Apple under Apple’s policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at
[Still Support](https://github.com/masuda-so/still/issues). Do not include private
journal content, payment information, or other sensitive data in a public issue.

This policy may change when Still adds a new data practice. The effective date
above will be updated when that happens.
